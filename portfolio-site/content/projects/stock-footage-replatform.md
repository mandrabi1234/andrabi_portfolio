## Overview

Sony Pictures' content licensing team manages a library of stock footage records. I replatformed
the database behind it from defunct legacy technology to a modern, industry-standard database
system, acting as project lead and principal engineer.

## The problem

The legacy system was effectively unsupported, and the work around it was entirely manual.
Importing, locating, cataloguing and maintaining records took hours, and keeping the old platform
running carried ongoing cost.

## What I built

- **New UI.** A bespoke interface designed around how the licensing team actually works.
- **Migration.** Automated data migration and cleanup from the legacy system.
- **Cataloguing.** A largely automated cataloguing workflow that replaced manual entry.

I handled the full lifecycle myself: ideation and requirements gathering with the licensing team,
design, development, deployment, and ongoing support for the people using it.

## Impact

- Cataloguing went from hours of manual work to a few minutes per 100 records.
- Lower cost to maintain the platform, and fewer staff hours spent on upkeep.
