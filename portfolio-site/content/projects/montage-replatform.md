## Overview

Montage supports Sony Pictures' Creative Affairs and Business Affairs teams. The project moved
their operations and data management off a defunct legacy application and onto a modern system
built on the Salesforce stack.

## My role

During the replatform I supported development on the new platform while working directly with
business users on day-to-day operations. Since the initial deployment I have taken over as the
main developer, leading development and architectural enhancements while continuing to support
the teams who use it.
