## Overview

PASS is a performance evaluation and team-building platform for soccer programs that don't have
an analytics department. Coaches get model-driven evaluations of their players and team without
manually tagging match footage.

## The problem

College and club programs rarely have the staff or budget for professional tracking data, so
player evaluation depends on memory and hand-kept stats.

## What I built

- **Models.** Proprietary predictive models that evaluate team and athlete performance.
- **Ingestion.** A computer-vision pipeline built on YOLO that extracts player and match data from
  footage, so the dataset grows on its own as programs upload games.
- **Web application.** A full-stack Flask, SQL and React.js app where coaches explore the results.
- **Analyst-in-a-box.** A GPT-powered chat interface that answers questions about the data and
  generates custom player performance reports.

## Impact

Licensed by D3 collegiate and club programs for in-season use.
